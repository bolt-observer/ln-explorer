Atlas · high-fidelity

bolt.observer as a map-first instrument. Every number, alias and graph position below comes from a snapshot of the live API taken on 24 Oct 2026 (data.js), laid out with the same ForceAtlas2 library the site already uses.

01 · Logo concepts

The yellow dome is replaced by a mark that works in one colour and holds up at 16px. Every concept is shown at 64px, 22px and as a favicon. Concept A is chosen and is now web/logo.svg.

02 · Foundations

Near-black canvas, one mint accent kept for selection and action only, coral reserved for negative deltas. IBM Plex Sans for interface text, Plex Mono with tabular figures for every number. Panels are translucent glass over the map: hairline borders, no drop shadows on content.

03 · Map, idle

The graph runs edge to edge. Labels only appear on the largest hubs and never overlap. Hover shows a tooltip and highlights the node. The network card replaces the old footer and the "Min channels" input.

04 · Search palette

Press ⌘K or / from anywhere. One box takes an alias, pubkey, SCID or NxNxN. ↵ flies the map to the node and opens the inspector; shift ↵ goes straight to the full profile.

05 · Node selected

The camera pans to the node, its neighbourhood lights up and everything else dims. The inspector shows the three numbers that matter, the largest peers and the fee position. The URL becomes /?node=03864e…, so the view can be shared.

06 · Channel selected

Clicking an edge explains the channel in terms of direction: who charges what to send which way, and what routing 100k sats actually costs. The cost is calculated from the two fee policies.

07 · Node profile

"Open full profile" zooms into the node. The hero is ACINQ's real peer set (1,605 peers, arranged by capacity). The 76-page table becomes a summary with the full list behind a tab.

08 · Nodes sheet

The table is a sheet over the map. Hovering a row shows that node on the map behind it. Filter chips replace the old form builder, and the defaults drop to six columns.

09 · Network stats sheet

One chart with a metric switcher instead of three stacked panels. Source caveats move into an ⓘ popover. Empty sections (opens and closes) shrink to one line until there is data.

10 · Mobile

One floating search bar instead of three header rows, and the standard map-app bottom sheet.

Map. The sheet peeks with a summary and drags up into the profile. Tables become card lists inside it.

Search. Opens full screen with recent lookups and suggested jumps. The keyboard stays up.
